READING THE RECORDS

Definitions and limits

What the labels mean

Sourced A number read from a primary record.

Calculated Arithmetic using linked inputs. The formula is available with the figure.

Estimate An explicit assumption, kept separate from recorded figures. No missing observations are estimated in this snapshot.

Budget, forecast and actual describe the financial basis. A sourced budget is not an actual payment. Missing or suppressed data are never zero.

Make like-for-like comparisons

FY25 means July 2024 through June 2025. Enrollment uses October 1 counts. FY13–FY18 aggregate K–12 school/grade rows in TDOE membership files, excluding pre-K. Later years use the ASR enrollment column. ADA and ADM are not substituted.

Operating spending includes funding from all sources. Total reported spending uses the ASR Grand Total of All Expenditures (Table 48 through FY16, Table 49 afterward). Older operating totals have a different definition, flagged in the historical view. Do not add municipal support to spending, which already reflects use of district resources.

Municipal support is the baseline operating contribution, excluding capital, settlements, debt service, tax pass-throughs and in-kind services. Budget and actual bases remain visible. Memphis–Shelby and Tipton are county benchmarks. They remain visible for this measure with a not-applicable label.

Pay, reserves and student outcomes

Pay compares regular teachers with a bachelor’s degree and zero experience. Select verified 10-month contracts for the closest contract comparison. Unknown contract terms stay out of normalized views. Payment installments do not determine contract length.

Fund balance is the exact General Purpose “Fund Balance” column in the ASR financial summary (Table 51 through FY16, Table 52 afterward). Unresolved FY16 figures and unavailable FY23 records remain blank. Reserves are separate and district classifications vary. It is not audited unrestricted cash. Inspect a figure for the separately reported reserve amount.

TCAP proficiency is the state’s published combined met/exceeded percentage for standard grades 3–8 tests, All Students. EOC and alternate assessments are separate and excluded here. FY2019 and FY2021–FY2026 are incorporated. The spring FY2020 gap remains blank. Other outcomes include federal cohort graduation rates, a separate state/cohort graduation series, and TVAAS growth. Published standards and pandemic-era conditions vary. No growth value is filled into the FY20 gap. Different student populations and district grade spans limit comparisons. These figures do not establish a causal relationship between funding and results.
