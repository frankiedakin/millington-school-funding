Reading these results

Each year tests different students. These are proficiency comparisons, not TVAAS growth scores or a measure of individual student growth. The combined grades 3–8 values are published state rates, not an average of the six grade percentages. They differ from the TISA measures combining certain grades with high-school courses. 2021 was a pandemic-era school year. The dashboard flags math EOC standard-setting changes in 2024, limiting comparisons across that change.

The district’s statement uses third-grade 2021 baselines of 18.4% reading and 12.6% math. The state dashboard shows 17.1% and 11.2%. This source or population difference has not been reconciled. Both are preserved and attributed.
